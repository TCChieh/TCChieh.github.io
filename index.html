About Me

I'm a Ph.D. student in the Taiwan International Graduate Program for Earth System Science, a joint program of Academia Sinica and National Taiwan University. My doctoral research builds digital twins of disaster events, starting with modeling human evacuation during a landslide-dam flood in eastern Taiwan.

Before that, I worked on human mobility and environmental big data: using highway ETC records to measure excess commuting and jobs-housing balance in Taiwan's metropolitan areas, and analyzing multi-year PM2.5 and PM1 data from community sensor networks. I also build WebGIS platforms and interactive dashboards that make spatial data easier to explore.

I'm happy to connect about collaborations and research opportunities.
